Privacy Policy

SSANZ Judge Log · Last updated 5 November 2026

In short: your judging records are saved in Google Drive, in your own Judging Record. The app has no server or database of its own, does not track you, and never sells or shares your information.

Who we are

The SSANZ Judge Log is provided by the SSANZ Judges Development Subcommittee (JDSC) to help judges keep their judging records. It is hosted at tonka-ssanz.github.io/judge-log. Contact: ssanzjdsubcommittee@gmail.com.

What information the app collects

The app only holds what you enter yourself, such as:

  • details of your judging appointments, for example the trial, date, location and classes judged
  • training records, such as stewarding with intent, probationary judging and mentoring appointments
  • your written or spoken reflections
  • a link to your own YouTube channel, and links to videos you have posted there

It does not collect your location, contacts, photos or any other information from your device.

How we use your Google account

You sign in with Google so the app can save your records to Google Drive. The app asks for one permission: to see and change only the Drive files it creates, or files you choose yourself with Google's file picker (such as your Judging Record). It cannot see or change any other files in your Drive.

Your Google information is used only to save and read your judging records for you. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone else.

The SSANZ Judge Log's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where your information is stored

  • Google Drive: your records are saved to your Judging Record in Google Drive. Who can see that file depends on how it is shared in Drive; your Judging Record may be visible to the JDSC administrators who manage judges' records.
  • Your device: while you have no internet, entries are kept in your browser on that device until they can be saved to Drive. Once saved, they stay on the device only so the app can work offline.
  • YouTube: videos stay on your own YouTube channel. The app only stores links, to your channel and to individual videos, so they can be opened from your record. It does not sign in to YouTube or access your YouTube account, and SSANZ does not store your videos.

The app has no server or database of its own and uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

Voice reflections

If you use the voice option, your phone or browser turns your speech into text using its built-in speech service (for example from Google or Apple). Only the resulting text is saved to your record. No audio is stored by the app.

Removing access or your information

  • You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To remove information kept on a device, sign out of the app or clear this website's data in your browser settings.
  • To have your Judging Record corrected or removed, contact the JDSC at the email below.

Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see, and to correct, the personal information held about you. Contact us at ssanzjdsubcommittee@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
